Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence 
 
MATAI 2027 — the International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial 
Intelligence — provides an international platform for researchers, scientists, engineers, 
academics, technology experts, and industry professionals to exchange knowled ge and present 
advances across the rapidly evolving fields of materials science, engineering, and artificial 
intelligence. 
 
The conference focuses on the convergence of advanced materials research, engineering 
innovation, computational methodologies, and artificial intelligence, with particular emphasis on 
emerging approaches to materials discovery, design, modelling, characterization, manufacturing, 
and sustainable technological development. 
 

===== PAGE 4 =====
MATAI 2027 brings together diverse scientific perspectives to examine how data -driven 
methods, machine learning, computational materials science, and intelligent engineering are 
reshaping the way advanced materials and next -generation technologies are rese arched and 
developed. 
 
Through keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral and poster sessions, technical 
discussions, and interdisciplinary scientific exchange, the conference aims to foster meaningful 
dialogue across academic, research, and industrial communities and to advance collaboration in 
emerging areas of materials and AI-enabled engineering. 
 
October 13–14, 2027 | Berlin, Germany 
 
## About the Conference 
 
MatAi 2027 — International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial 
Intelligence** will be held on **October 13–14, 2027, in Berlin, Germany**, bringing together an 
international community of researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, and i ndustry 
professionals working across materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence. 
 
The conference is dedicated to examining emerging developments at the intersection of 
**advanced materials research, engineering innovation, computational science, and artificial 
intelligence**. It provides a multidisciplinary platform for presenting signi ficant research 
findings, exchanging scientific knowledge, and discussing evolving methodologies and 
technologies that are shaping the future of materials development. 
 
Particular emphasis is placed on the growing application of **artificial intelligence, machine 
learning, materials informatics, computational modelling, and data -driven approaches** to 
materials discovery, design, characterization, processing, and manufact uring. The conference 
also encompasses developments in advanced materials, nanomaterial’s, polymers and 
composites, metals and alloys, ceramics, biomaterials, energy materials, smart materials, and 
sustainable materials engineering. 
 
**MatAi 2027** welcomes **original research, technical contributions, experimental and 
theoretical studies, computational investigations, and interdisciplinary perspectives** from 
academia, research institutions, and industry. Through keynote and plenary lectures , invited 
presentations, oral and poster sessions, and scientific discussions, the conference aims to 
facilitate meaningful exchange across disciplines and encourage international research 
collaboration.

Aim* 
MatAi 2027** aims to provide an international scientific platform for advancing knowledge and 
fostering interdisciplinary exchange at the convergence of **Materials Science, Engineering, and 
Artificial Intelligence**. The conference brings together researchers, academics, engineers, and 
industry professionals to examine emerging scientific developments, methodologies, and 
technologies shaping the future of materials research and engineering.

1. ABSTRACT SUBMISSION GUIDELINES 
Call for Abstracts 
MatAi 2027 welcomes original scientific and technical contributions covering materials 
science, engineering, artificial intelligence, computational research, materials informatics, 
advanced manufacturing, and related interdisciplinary fields. 
Abstracts may be submitted for consideration under the following presentation categories: 
• Keynote Presentation 
• Plenary Presentation 
• Invited Presentation 
• Oral Presentation 
• Poster Presentation 
Submissions should present original research, significant technical findings, experimental or 
theoretical studies, computational investigations, methodological developments, or relevant 
interdisciplinary work. 
Abstract Preparation 
Authors should prepare abstracts using the following structure: 
Title 
A concise and informative title reflecting the research. 
Authors & Affiliations 
Full names of all authors, institutional affiliations, city, and country. 
Background / Research Context 
Briefly describe the scientific context and research problem. 
Objectives 
Clearly state the principal objectives or research questions. 

===== PAGE 75 =====
Methodology 
Summarize the experimental, theoretical, computational, analytical, or data-driven methods 
used. 
Results / Findings 
Present the principal results, observations, or scientific findings. 
Conclusion 
Summarize the main conclusions and scientific significance. 
Keywords 
Provide approximately 3–6 relevant keywords. 
Submission Requirements 
• Abstracts should be written in clear scientific English. 
• The title should accurately represent the submitted research. 
• Author names and affiliations should be checked carefully. 
• Submitted work should be original. 
• Plagiarized, fabricated, or misleading content will not be accepted. 
• References may be included where appropriate. 
• Tables, figures, equations, or special formatting should follow the submission 
instructions. 
• The presenting author should be clearly identified. 
• Abstracts should be submitted through the official submission process. 
Abstract Review 
Submitted abstracts may be evaluated based on: 
• Scientific quality 
• Originality 
• Relevance to the conference scope 
• Technical significance 
• Clarity 
• Methodological soundness 
• Potential contribution to the field 
The scientific committee may accept an abstract, request clarification or revision, recommend 
an alternative presentation category, or decline a submission that does not meet the 
conference requirements. 
Abstract submission does not guarantee acceptance or a specific presentation category.
