Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

MATAI 2027 — the International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence — provides an international platform for researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology experts, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge and present advances across the rapidly evolving fields of materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The conference focuses on the convergence of advanced materials research, engineering innovation, computational methodologies, and artificial intelligence, with particular emphasis on emerging approaches to materials discovery, design, modelling, characterization, manufacturing, and sustainable technological development.

MATAI 2027 brings together diverse scientific perspectives to examine how data-driven methods, machine learning, computational materials science, and intelligent engineering are reshaping the way advanced materials and next-generation technologies are researched and developed.

Through keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral and poster sessions, technical discussions, and interdisciplinary scientific exchange, the conference aims to foster meaningful dialogue across academic, research, and industrial communities and to advance collaboration in emerging areas of materials and AI-enabled engineering.
